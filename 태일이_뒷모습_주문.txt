------------------------------------------------------------
The attached Image 1 is our finished FRONT picture of Taichi Yagami (Tai Kamiya) from Digimon Adventure, in color pixel art. Image 2 is his small walking sheet; its middle row shows the back of his head and his back.

Task: draw his BACK battle sprite for a Nintendo DS Pokemon HeartGold/SoulSilver style battle screen (the player trainer seen from behind, like the player's back sprite in those games), in exactly the same pixel style and the same colors as Image 1: chunky square pixels, thick black outline, flat colors, no gradients, no anti-aliasing.

Pose: three-quarter view from behind, slightly turned toward the upper right, so we see his back, the back of his head, one shoulder, and only a thin sliver of the side of his face (cheek and ear edge, no eyes). One arm is bent forward as if he is about to throw something or hold up his Digivice.

Keep from behind:
- the big spiky brown hair mass, bigger than his head, with sharp spikes sticking out to the sides and up
- the blue strap of the goggles running across the back of his head, and the white goggles peeking out above the hair edge on both sides
- the blue short-sleeve T-shirt (plain back), bare arms, white gloves
- brown cargo shorts at the bottom edge

Framing: one character, big, centered, filling the whole square canvas from the top of his hair down to the middle of his thighs; the lower legs are cut off by the bottom edge. Plain solid white background, no ground, no shadow, no text, no frame.

Draw it on a coarse grid of about 80x80 pixels (each pixel a big visible block), so it can be shrunk to 80x80 later without blur. Output one square image.
------------------------------------------------------------
